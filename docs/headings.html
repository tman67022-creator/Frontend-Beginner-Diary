Testing Out the Heading Tags

HTML provides six different heading levels, from <h1> to <h6>. They are used to organize the content of a webpage, with <h1> being the most important heading and <h6> being the least important.

Explanation

Heading tags are not just for making text bigger or smaller. They define the structure of a webpage, making it easier for browsers, search engines, and screen readers to understand the content.
